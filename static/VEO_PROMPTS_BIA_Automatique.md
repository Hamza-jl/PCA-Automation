##  HOW TO USE THESE PROMPTS

Each prompt is designed for **Google Veo** (8-second clips).

**Before generating:**
1. Upload your **character reference image** — Veo will use it to maintain identity consistency.
2. Upload your **location/place reference image** — this anchors the spatial setting across all scenes.
3. Copy-paste the prompt for each scene.
4. For best coherence, generate scenes **in order**, using the previous clip as a reference frame where Veo allows it.

##  VOICEOVER PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Language:** French — professional, neutral, slight journalistic cadence
- **Pacing:** ~20–22 words per 8-second clip (slow enough to breathe, fast enough to feel dynamic)
- **Tone arc:** Calm & expert (S1–S4) → Heavy & factual (S5–S6) → Resolved & proud (S7)
- **Recommended voice profile:** Male or female, 30–45 years old, francophone, no regional accent, clean studio recording
- **If using AI TTS:** ElevenLabs "Callum" or "Charlotte" (French), or Google WaveNet `fr-FR-Neural2-D`
- **Sync tip:** Record the voiceover first, then trim each Veo clip's duration to match the audio, or use CapCut/Premiere to align
